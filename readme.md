# Yahoo Fantasy GM Platform

An evolving fantasy basketball intelligence platform designed to help fantasy managers make smarter, data-driven decisions.

## Overview

The Yahoo Fantasy GM Platform aims to turn fantasy basketball data into actionable insights. By combining player performance, roster information, league activity, and upcoming schedules, the platform will help managers understand player value, discover emerging opportunities, and make better roster decisions.

The long-term vision is to build a league-wide intelligence system that supports every team while providing a centralized dashboard for exploring player trends and league insights.

## Key Capabilities

- **Player Intelligence:** Analyze player performance, trends, and potential value.
- **League Insights:** Explore team rosters, player ownership, and league-wide activity.
- **Performance Analytics:** Identify top performers across different time periods, from daily standouts to monthly leaders.
- **Waiver Wire Intelligence:** Discover valuable available players and potential breakout candidates.
- **Fantasy GM Advisor:** Recommend roster improvements, including weekly add/drop decisions, for teams across the league.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Bring player rankings, performance trends, and actionable recommendations into one place.

## Current Progress

The project has established its initial data foundation through Yahoo Fantasy API integration, roster data collection, structured data extraction, and roster validation.

Development is ongoing. The current focus is expanding beyond roster data toward a broader player dataset and the analytics needed to support league-wide insights and recommendations.

## Future Direction

The platform will evolve incrementally, guided by data quality, analytical usefulness, and practical fantasy basketball needs.

Potential areas of development include historical player performance, statistical leaderboards, player availability tracking, weekly projections, personalized roster recommendations, and an interactive dashboard.

The implementation and priorities may change as the project develops.

## Development Philosophy

- **Data-driven:** Ground insights in reliable, relevant data.
- **Action-oriented:** Translate analysis into practical fantasy decisions.
- **League-wide:** Support decision-making across all teams, not just one roster.
- **Explainable:** Make recommendations understandable and evidence-based.
- **Iterative:** Build, validate, and improve capabilities incrementally.

## Project Status

This is an active work in progress. Current functionality, architecture, and future priorities will evolve as new capabilities are developed and validated.
